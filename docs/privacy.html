Privacy Policy — Benchmark Prep FS

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Benchmark Prep FS (“the app”) is an independent study tool for the Fundamentals of Surveying exam. This policy describes what the app does with your information. The short version: the app has no user accounts and does not collect your personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask you to sign up, sign in, or provide a name, email address, or any other personal information, and it does not transmit your study activity to us or to any third party.

Information stored on your device

Your progress — answers, scores, streaks, review schedule, and settings — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data. We cannot access it, recover it, or restore it for you.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Children

The app is designed for adults preparing for a professional licensing exam. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds optional features that change how data is handled (for example, optional sign-in and sync), this policy will be updated and the effective date revised before those features ship.
